About Me

Hi, I'm Jiaqi Kevin Yang. I am currently completing a MSc in Computer Science at McGill University. I previously completed my BSc in Software Engineering at McGill. During my undergraduate years, I also did a student exchange semester at Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

Professionally, I have worked as a QA Automation intern at dormakaba where I worked on expanding test suites for a smart lock product. I currently mentor over 200 students as a Teaching Assistant in computer architecture and assembly at McGill University.

I am a software engineer proficient in a diverse set of programming languages, including Java, Python, C/C++, C#, and JavaScript/TypeScript. When I step away from the keyboard, I'm usually out working up a sweat. I love long-distance bikepacking (recently completing a tour across most of Hokkaido) and hiking a good mountain. I am also a big fan of badminton and bouldering.
